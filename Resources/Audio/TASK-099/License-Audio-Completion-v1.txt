PROJECT-CREATED COMPONENTS

The fire and wind loops are original deterministic procedural sound designs
created for this Hearthward task. They contain no third-party audio recordings.
The added landing body/grit layers are likewise procedural. Revision 2 water
cues contain only edited identified CC0 water recordings; no procedural layers.
The editable generator, parameters, exact source hashes, per-cue seeds and
technical measurements are retained in art_source/TASK-099/audio-completion-v1/.
No paid library, restricted stock source, TTS, spoken voice, music composition,
or cloned performance was used. There are no additional third-party sample
license conditions for these procedural components. This notice does not impose
a new open-source license on the game or claim exclusive copyright in AI-assisted
material. Existing project ownership and distribution arrangements are unchanged.

TECHNICAL AND ACCEPTANCE BOUNDARIES

All five files: single-channel 48000 Hz, 16-bit little-endian PCM WAV.
Fire/wind use circular synthesis, including wrapped transient tails, and retain
headroom for the runtime mix. Format, clipping, boundary and deterministic-render
checks do not establish subjective quality or in-engine/device listening approval.
Fixed dialogue remains unproduced. No silent substitute voice file was created.
