The creator describes actual bucket-water experiments recorded with a webcam.
These are water-action Foley recordings, not claimed as a person entering or
exiting a natural lake. Edits select different natural splash/drain windows,
remove pre-roll, lightly filter recording rumble/hiss, apply short edge fades,
and set independent levels. No generated bubble tones, synthesized water noise,
pitch shifting, reversal, human voice, or TTS is added in the revised water cues.
